1. Examination Details

All-India Common Assessment Test (AICAT - 2026)

The National Academic Examination Board conducts the All-India Common Assessment Test for admission into undergraduate Engineering, Sciences, and Management programs across affiliated institutions.

  • Examination Mode: Computer-Based Test (CBT) at designated centers
  • Duration: 180 Minutes (3 Hours)
  • Registration Window: November 1, 2026 to December 15, 2026
  • Application Fee: INR 1,200 (General/OBC) | INR 600 (SC/ST/PwD)

3. Eligibility Criteria

  • Candidate must have passed or be appearing in Class 12 / Pre-University Examination from a recognized board.
  • Minimum aggregate of 60% marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (55% for reserved categories).
  • Candidates born on or after October 1, 2003 are eligible to apply.

4. Mandatory Examination Instructions

  1. Report to the examination center at least 60 minutes prior to the scheduled start time.
  2. Carry a printed copy of the QR-enabled Hall Ticket along with an original government photo ID (Aadhaar / Passport).
  3. Electronic gadgets, smartwatches, programmable calculators, and notes are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall.
  4. Use only the rough sheets provided by the invigilator inside the CBT laboratory.
